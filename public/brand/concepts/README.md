# TopUpSend logo concepts

Three concepts, each as an icon (64x64) and a lockup (icon + wordmark).
SVG is the source of truth. Awaiting the owner's pick; the chosen concept
gets a full asset pack (favicon/app icons, dark variant) and replaces the
placeholder Zap tile in `src/components/PublicNavbar.tsx`.

- **A — Send Bolt** (`concept-a-*`): paper plane on an indigo tile (#4F46E5).
  "Send" at a glance; sharpest at favicon sizes.
- **B — Top Arrow** (`concept-b-*`): a bold T topped by an emerald up-arrow
  on slate (#0F172A / #10B981). "Top up" literally.
- **C — Globe Plus** (`concept-c-*`): globe with a top-up plus badge
  (sky #0284C7 / amber #F59E0B). "Global top-up"; busiest at 16px.
